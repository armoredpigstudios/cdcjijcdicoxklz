About

Can you solve the mystery and survive?

Every round you get a secret role. Find out who the Murderer is... before it's too late!

Innocent

Run, hide and grab coins. Figure out who the Murderer is!

Sheriff

You have the gun. Shoot the Murderer. But if you shoot an innocent, you go down too!

Murderer

You have the knife. Get EVERYONE. Don't get shot!

Hero

If the Sheriff goes down, an Innocent can grab the gun and save the day!

What's in it

  • Move like a gorilla: swing and leap with your arms
  • Vote on the map every round
  • Collect coins and finish daily quests
  • Unlock knife and gun skins
  • Make a private room for you and your friends
  • Dead? Become a ghost and keep watching

Find the Murderer. Trust no monkey!
