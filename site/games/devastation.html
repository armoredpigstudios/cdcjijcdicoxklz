About

Shoot out the supports. Watch the tower fall.

DEVASTATION VR is a destruction sandbox. Everything built breaks into chunks, buildings collapse when their supports go, towers topple whole and fire spreads.

Bring it down

  • The Annihilator laser cuts through buildings
  • Railgun, tesla gun, black-hole launcher, scoped sniper
  • C4, napalm, cluster bombs, airstrikes and a nuke

Start a war

  • Red vs Blue: about a hundred soldiers in squads, with tanks and jets
  • Cops vs criminals vs the mafia
  • Join a side, or let them fight each other and you

15 monsters

Gorgonzola, King Chonk, Cthulhu, Mothzarella, a T-Rex and more, and they fight everyone.

17 vehicles

A tank, a mech, a jet bomber, a wrecking crane, a bulldozer that plows through houses and the Fatcycle, a bat-bike that flies.

12 big maps

An Airport full of airliners, a Theme Park, a villain's volcano, Prison Island in a storm, a Moon Base in low gravity and more.

Play solo, or in private co-op by room code with voice chat. Late-90s console looks, on purpose, and an original breakbeat soundtrack.
