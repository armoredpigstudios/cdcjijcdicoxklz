About

Someone in the room is a murderer. Nobody knows who.

Each round, one player is secretly handed the knife and another gets the sheriff's gun. Everyone else is innocent, with three minutes to stay alive.

The murderer

Draw the blade from your hip and get close, or flick your wrist and throw it down a corridor. It comes back to your sheath a moment later. Take everyone out before the clock runs out.

The sheriff

One gun, one job: work out who the killer is and shoot first.

The innocents

Run, hide and watch who's acting strange. If the sheriff goes down, the gun drops, and any innocent brave enough to grab it becomes the hero.

Ten maps

Ravenholt Manor, the Pier 9 docks, an office forty floors up, St Agatha's hospital, Tinkerton Toy Works, Hollow Lane on Halloween, Blackwater Penitentiary, the dungeons under Castle Grimhold, Maple Grove Plaza and a college dorm. Vote on the next map between rounds.

Play together

Jump into a public room of up to 12 players, or make a private room and share the code with friends.

Make it yours

Knife skins with auras, trails and kill effects, gun skins with coloured shots, and a rack of hats. Optional role tokens let you choose to be the murderer or the sheriff next round.
