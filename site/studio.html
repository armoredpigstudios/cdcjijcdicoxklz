About

Armored Pig Studios is a VR game studio based in America.

We love social multiplayer games, the kind where the best moments come from whoever else is in the room, so everything we make can be played together. Half the fun of Murder Mystery is working out who's holding the knife. Buddy Beatdown is at its best with a lobby full of friends and far too many explosives.

Most of our games have public rooms to drop into, private rooms you join with a code, and voice chat.

Our own games:

  • Prison Break, where inmates plan a breakout and cops try to stop them
  • Murder Monkey, a murder mystery where you move like a gorilla
  • DEVASTATION, a destruction sandbox in Early Access where buildings come down in chunks
  • Murder Mystery, a social deduction game for up to 12 players
  • Web Weaver, a physics-based web-swinging game

Buddy Beatdown is made with 2080 Games and gets a big free update every month.
